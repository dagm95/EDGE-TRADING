About

Excellence in Interior Design and Craftsmanship

At Edge Trading Plc, we pride ourselves on being a leader in interior design, finishing, and craftsmanship. Our commitment to quality and innovation drives us to create spaces that are not only functional but also inspiring. We understand that every project is unique, and we strive to exceed our clients' expectations through personalized service and meticulous attention to detail. As we navigate the complexities of design and construction, we remain dedicated to our core values of integrity, reliability, and sustainability. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients and partners, fostering collaboration that leads to remarkable results. With a focus on continuous improvement, we embrace the latest technologies and creative solutions in both interior design and finishing, ensuring that our work not only meets but sets industry standards. Our vision is to be synonymous with excellence in craftsmanship, where every decision is guided by honesty and a commitment to customer satisfaction. Join us on this journey as we shape the future of our industry, uplift our communities, and empower our team to lead with creativity and purpose. Together, we can create spaces that truly reflect your vision

Mission

At Edge Trading Plc, our mission is to deliver high-quality metal and wood works—along with exceptional interior design and finishing—through innovation, integrity, and on-time delivery. We are committed to exceeding client expectations by embracing new technologies, fostering creativity, and building lasting partnerships based on trust and transparency. With a focus on sustainability and social responsibility, we strive to make a positive impact on both our community and environment, while empowering our team to grow, lead, and excel.

Vision

Our vision at Edge Trading Plc. is to be Ethiopia’s leading name in quality craftsmanship and innovation—trusted across industries for delivering reliable, forward-thinking solutions. We aspire to set new benchmarks in metal and wood works, interior design, and finishing by combining integrity, creativity, and a deep understanding of our clients’ needs. We envision Edge Trading as a symbol of excellence—where honesty drives every decision, and customer satisfaction is not just a goal but a guarantee. Through continuous improvement, cutting-edge technology, and a strong commitment to our people, we aim to shape the future of our industry while empowering our team and uplifting the communities we serve.

Values

1. Integrity We operate with honesty, transparency, and accountability in every relationship and decision.
2. Quality Excellence We are committed to delivering top-tier craftsmanship and service without compromise.
3. Innovation We embrace new technologies and creative thinking to deliver modern, effective solutions.
4. Reliability We keep our promises—delivering on time, every time, with consistency and dependability.
5. Customer Focus We put our clients first, striving to understand and exceed their expectations.
6. Teamwork & Collaboration We believe great results come from working together—with our colleagues, clients, and partners.
7. Continuous Improvement We constantly refine our skills, processes, and approaches to stay ahead of the curve.
8. Sustainability & Responsibility We are committed to ethical practices that positively impact our community and environment.
9. Empowerment & Growth We invest in our people, encouraging creativity, leadership, and personal development.
